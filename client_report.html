Reflection

I feel like this project was a good reminder to me that while there are standards and best practices, there will always be stuff that will get in the way. You just need to find a way to work with it. With my current work I completed a data table for a city 3 weeks ago and because of how busy everybody is, it will probably be another month before the first draft is reviewed. I think this project has been insightful to me because there were times when my lack of speed slowed everybody else down. There were other times when my team either didn't respond or something happened that prevented me from getting what I neeeded to do. At the end of the day, I feel like while my HTML and CSS has improved, the biggest thing I learned was how to work with other people and how to deal with slowdowns in a meaningful way.
